# Musia Agent And Community Request

Owner's requested direction, 2026-10-09:

1. Add music-generation agent mode to Musia.
2. Reuse LazyingArt login/invitations, with Apple, Google and GitHub where
   qualified; learn from AiMemo, EchoMind, OnlyIdeasApp, Bunko and
   LazyingArtLinkPrivate.
3. Enforce per-person usage and offer three subscription tiers.
4. Let creators keep music private or share it publicly, with hearts, likes,
   comments, saves and share links, learning from OnlyIdeas' recent community work.
5. Core principle: listening to public music and social participation stay free;
   generation is the metered capability.

The owner subsequently approved Free (2 renders/month), Creator (20 renders at
US$9.99/month) and Studio (80 renders at US$29.99/month) as launch targets.
Private creations are allowed on every tier. Paid checkout still requires
app-owned products, receipt verification and qualification before activation.

Implementation, reuse evidence, source/pilot limitations, tests and next release
gates: [Musia Creator](../docs/musia-creator-agent.md).

Keep the existing music quality principles: audible beautiful singing, musical
space rather than crammed lyrics, input-aware ASR correction, exact selected
audio/timing binding, and no automatic public release of an unreviewed render.
Never publish the agent conversation, private prompt or operator notes as song
description/lyrics. Account identity does not grant another app's subscription.
