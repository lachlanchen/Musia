# 行遍世间 - Production And Provenance Note

## Source Finding

The sentence `我行遍世间所有的路……` does not lead to a stable authenticated
full poem. Searchable pages embed it in inconsistent modern internet texts and
attribute those texts differently. A discoverable fiction page also uses the
expanded sentence, but that does not establish original authorship:

- https://www.jjwxc.net/onebook.php?novelid=2705850
- https://www.yasong.net/14345.html

Musia therefore did not reproduce a circulating “full version” or label it as a
work by Cangyang Gyatso. The short user-supplied phrase was treated as a seed for
a new original song about travel, missed time, recognition, and homecoming.

## Mandatory Post-Correction

The website lyric is based on what the selected audio actually sings. The audit
uses the full mix and Demucs vocal stem, large-v3 normal/no-VAD ASR, MOSS-Music,
and the planned lyric as a reference rather than as truth.

Two planned lines were not sung and are not published. Sound-close substitutions
are restored to the intended lyric only when phonetics, syllable count, context,
and multiple evidence routes agree. The shortened generated outro is represented
as `原来，在身边` rather than copying unsung draft words.
