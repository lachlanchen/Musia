# ベイビー、そのままで · Baby, Just as You Are

Artist: Musia. A Japanese indie-pop song with a warm female vocal.

She takes a shirt out of the cupboard, catches her reflection, and hesitates.
Today she puts it on anyway. Her friends are getting together for a photo.
This time someone saves her a spot right in the middle. And on a night when
even smiling feels like work, the seat beside that friend is still hers.

「数字ひとつじゃ はかれない、君と笑った この世界。」
鏡の前で迷った朝も、好きな色で出かけよう。写真の真ん中、空けてあるよ。

宝贝，就这样很好。你和我一起笑过的日子，哪里是一个数字能衡量的。
