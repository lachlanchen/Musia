# 楓の向こうで · Beyond the Maples

Artist: Musia
Vocal language: Japanese
Genre: warm, bittersweet J-pop ballad

Aya is travelling in Canada. Autumn photographs arrive while the person who
misses her is awake on the other side of the world. At home, two coffee cups
still appear on the table out of habit. The song holds two feelings together:
"I miss you" and "Take your time; see the places you have not seen yet."
The souvenirs that matter are her stories, and the ending imagines holding
her again without needing any words.
