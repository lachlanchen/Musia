Aya is coming home from Canada to Japan. A red maple leaf lies between the
pages of her notebook; the suitcase is full, and someone is waiting beyond
the station gates. She takes off her down coat. Chiba's cool breeze brushes
her cheeks, and the familiar words "ただいま / おかえり" turn the long journey
into the simple joy of being together again.

This is a bright, warm Japanese pop song about reunion, ordinary happiness,
and the feeling that home can be a person. Its last image is walking side by
side, with travel stories left for later. The emotional invitation is for
anyone who has missed someone across an ocean and imagined the first hello.
