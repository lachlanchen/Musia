# Song Context

Official release title: せんせいも一年生 · A Beginner, Even Now
Chinese title: 老师也是一年级
Artist: Musia
Vocal language: Japanese
Character: あや / Aya / 阿芽
Listening page: https://fun.lazying.art/#sensei-mo-ichinensei

Aya reads the offer email three times. On the fourth, she finally finds her
voice. A new title feels exciting and a little too big; she practises saying
hello, only for her stomach to answer first. There are questions she cannot
solve, colorful notes on her desk, and room for one more chair beside her.
On the way home, a pudding is enough reason to celebrate.

This bright, playful Japanese piano-pop song is about becoming a teacher
without losing the courage to be a beginner. A job title is a starting point,
not a demand to know everything. Take a breath, bring yesterday's tears with
you, and meet tomorrow at your own pace.

阿芽把内定邮件看了三遍，第四遍才终于喊出声。练习自我介绍时，肚子先
替她打了招呼。桌上还有没解开的题，也可以再搬来一把椅子，一起慢慢想。
成为老师，并不意味着从此无所不知。回家的路上买一只布丁，今天也值得
好好庆祝。明天还没完成，我们也可以继续做一年级。

あや先生も、まだ一年生。答えの出ない問いには、椅子をもうひとつ。
背伸びのあとは深呼吸。帰り道のプリンと、小さな「はじめまして」を
連れて、明日へ歩いていく歌。
