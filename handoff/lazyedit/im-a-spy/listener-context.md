Release title: 我是间谍 · I'm a Spy
Artist: Musia
Vocal language: English. Japanese and Chinese on-screen text translate the song.
Style: dreamy, playful indie pop with a warm male vocal and a little mystery.

He calls himself a spy and tries to play it cool, but his eyes give him away.
Every secret comes alive when the person he loves walks past. Beneath the
starry sky, the bravest thing he can do is offer his hand: don't cry, we can
fly. It is an affectionate, slightly silly promise to stay, not a story about
surveillance or a real secret agent.
