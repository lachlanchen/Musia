Uplifting Mandarin Chinese guofeng pop-rock anthem, brave romantic journey and homecoming. Expressive clear female lead, warm intimate verses and a soaring powerful melodic chorus. Open with the sung 半首琴声 留在长安 hook, returning to the same instantly memorable chorus melody. Propulsive live drums, galloping bass, rhythmic pipa, electric guitar, sweeping strings and guqin accents. Spacious connected vocal phrases, strong an/ang vowel endings, a tender half-time bridge, triumphant final chorus, gentle complete instrumental resolution. 104 BPM, D minor, 4/4.
