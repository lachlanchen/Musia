# Baby, Just as You Are

Japanese title: **ベイビー、そのままで**.
Chinese meaning: **宝贝，就这样很好**. Artist: Musia.

A warm Japanese song about being loved without having to make one's body
smaller or fit another person's idea of beauty. "Baby" is an affectionate
nickname for an adult partner, not a child. The story moves from hesitation in
front of a mirror to a favourite shirt, a place in a photograph, and walking
into tomorrow. The empty neighbouring seat makes room for a difficult day too.
Neither thinness nor roundness is a measure of someone's worth.
