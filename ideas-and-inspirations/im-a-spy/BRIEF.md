# I'm a Spy

An affectionate, slightly surreal love song: someone playing it cool cannot
hide how much they care. The repeated spy / why / eye / cry sounds are its
musical centre. Keep the original simple charm rather than turning the song
into a literal surveillance story.

## Performance

my hand and we can fly" brings back the playfulness.

4/4. These are composition directions, not verified analysis.
