# せんせいも一年生 · A Beginner, Even Now

A joyful Japanese song about Aya receiving an offer to become a specially
appointed assistant professor in Chiba. Becoming a teacher does not end
curiosity: there are more questions, people to learn alongside, and pudding
on the way home. This is a fictional musical portrait, not a university
announcement or an assertion that an appointment has already started.

## Production Brief

Preserve the approved Japanese lyric. Use natural Japanese rather than romaji.
The phrase 特任助教 reads とくにんじょきょう; 千葉 reads ちば, 付箋 reads
ふせん, 一年生 reads いちねんせい. The protagonist is あや / 阿芽.
The formal job title is not 教授. Keep school imagery light and adult rather
than turning this into a children's song.

chorus, shared curiosity, a quiet self-kindness bridge, a bright final chorus.
