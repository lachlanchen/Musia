# パリへ、ひとつの勇気 · Brave in Paris

## Request

Aya-chan travels to Paris: the Louvre, Versailles, the Arc de Triomphe and the
opera. Write and generate a happy song celebrating her bravery and the person
missing her, then publish a website listening preview. No recording or social
publication is requested for this step.

## Musical Direction

Native Japanese, warm female lead, joyful travel pop with a tender bridge.
Reuse the proven ACE XL Turbo full-song route from Beyond the Maples and Home
on the Breeze. First sweep: 174 seconds and six seeds. Roomier sweep: 188
seconds and four seeds. Both use 118 BPM, G major and eight steps.
These are production directions, not measured score facts. No voice cloning.

The narrator wants Aya to explore, not rush home to soothe their loneliness.
The chorus repeats あや / パリの空 and 羽になる; small brave steps become wings.
Concrete details carry the emotion: a photograph, unfamiliar words, the wind
in Versailles, one dinner setting, and an unsent admission of missing her.
春を呼ぶ is a metaphor for warmth, not a claim about the travel season.

The source has 36 short lines with two repeated eight-line choruses. Native
Japanese preserves context; no pinyin or romaji is used as the singing input.
Longer phrases get space across bars. Avoid packing landmark explanations into
the caption or treating Japanese end-rhyme like compulsory English rhyme.
Check mora flow, breathing, and natural particles in the selected render.

Pronunciation reference: ルーヴル, ヴェルサイユ, 凱旋門=がいせんもん,
オペラ座=オペラざ, 石畳=いしだたみ, 羽=はね, 君=きみ, 内緒=ないしょ.

## Acceptance

Compare candidate signal health and musical screening, then transcribe the
selected vocal independently with large-v3 and a second music-aware route.
Preserve sound-close source words, but publish actual repeats/omissions and
entrances. Audit the complete outro and every source line. Website tracks must
translate the selected Japanese performance into English and Chinese, with
Japanese furigana and Chinese pinyin. Chords/beats belong to the selected audio
and remain analysis-grade. Create a fresh song-specific 16:9 Paris cover.
