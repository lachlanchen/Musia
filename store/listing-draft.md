## Listing Copy

Name: **Musia**

Apple subtitle: **Hear the beat. Play along.**

Google short description: **Practice guitar at your pace with song loops, chord shapes and a steady pulse.**

Description:

Start making sense of music, one small phrase at a time. Musia brings listening,
rhythm practice and guitar play-along into the same workspace. You do not need
to sing before you begin.

- Start with First Pulse, a short reference exercise using Em and Am.
- Listen, tap along, then practice a chord change at a comfortable speed.
- Slow down or speed up playback from 25% to 200% while preserving pitch.
- Replay a phrase and follow timed lyrics and available chord shapes.
- Explore a curated song library, including Japanese, Chinese and English vocals.
- Keep practice history on your device and export or clear it when you choose.

Automatically analyzed song chords, beats and melody are estimates, not a
verified score. Tap feedback measures your tapping, not guitar performance or
singing ability. The first release does not record your microphone, require an
account or offer cloud music generation. Streaming the song library requires an
internet connection.
