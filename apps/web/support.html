Something sounds wrong?

Include the song title, vocal version and approximate playback time. Song chords and note estimates can be wrong; a musical teacher's reviewed score takes priority. The Foundations exercise uses a known beat and chord sequence.

Playback

Check your device volume and network connection, then press play. Browser background playback depends on your browser and operating system. A slower playback speed preserves pitch where the playback engine supports it.

Practice history

History belongs to this device. Export it before clearing browser storage or reinstalling. Tap measurements are timing observations, not singing or guitar accuracy grades; Bluetooth and device latency can affect them.

Creation

Creative briefs can be saved locally. Cloud music generation, uploads and an AI chat coach are not enabled in the current learning release.
