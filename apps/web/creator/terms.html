Creator terms & privacy

Version 2026-10-09 · Creator pilot

Your music

Only submit lyrics or materials you own or are allowed to use. No unauthorized voice cloning, impersonation, harassment, hateful abuse, exploitation or deceptive attribution. Generated music is not guaranteed to be unique, copyrightable or free of third-party rights. Review it before sharing or commercial use.

Private by default

Local drafts stay on this browser until you submit them. Agent requests send your brief to the configured OpenAI or DeepSeek provider. Render requests are processed by Musia's private ACE worker. These requests are not published. Public sharing is a separate, explicit choice, and songs and comments await review. Likes are counted publicly; saved-song lists remain private.

Account and access

LazyingArt supplies your stable identity and display name. Musia does not receive your provider password. Signing in does not transfer subscriptions or private data from another LazyingArt app. During the creator pilot, invitations control generation, not public listening.

Usage

Free: 2 renders/month. Creator: 20 renders/month, planned US$9.99/month. Studio: 80 renders/month, planned US$29.99/month. Subscriptions are not currently sold in this pilot. One render is one audio candidate up to 3 minutes; a different language or new candidate is another render. Failed or quality-rejected jobs release their reservation. Requests awaiting review remain reserved. Limits reset each UTC calendar month, without rollover. Agent refinement and anti-abuse limits are separate.

Safety and removal

You can report public content, block an author, remove your comments and return your music to private visibility. Private means unavailable to other listeners; authorized operators can review content for safety and quality. Downloaded public copies cannot be recalled. Contact contact@lazying.art for copyright, safety or privacy requests.

Deletion

Deleting your Musia creator account immediately revokes app access and hides its songs. Associated private media require operator removal during this pilot. Minimal anti-abuse, deletion and financial records may be retained as necessary; there is no payment data in this pilot. Do not use the pilot for sensitive personal recordings.

Public listening and local music practice remain available without a creator subscription.
